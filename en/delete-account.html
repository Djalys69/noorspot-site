Delete my account

You can delete your NoorSpot account and personal data at any time, directly from the app or by writing to us.

From the app

  1. Open NoorSpot and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Settings.
  3. Tap “Delete my account” and confirm.

By email

Write to us from your account’s email address with the subject “Account deletion”. We handle the request within 30 days. contact@noorspot.com

What happens to my content?

Your personal data (account, profile, favourites, history) is erased. Places you added remain visible but anonymised. Subscriptions are not cancelled automatically: cancel them from the App Store or Google Play.
