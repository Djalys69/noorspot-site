How it works

  1. You send your request with your address, logo and offer.
  2. We review your listing (business suited to our community, compliant content).
  3. You receive an invoice by e-mail with a secure payment link (Stripe).
  4. After payment, your listing is published automatically and ends at the end of the chosen period.
