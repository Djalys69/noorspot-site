An app made with you

NoorSpot grows thanks to the people who use it. Every place added, every photo and every review helps another Muslim pray on time, close to home or while travelling.

  • Add a place in seconds and complete it with your photos.
  • Leave an honest review: an AI checks every piece of content and protects the community.
  • Earn “hasanettes” (good deeds) with every contribution.

Swimming: find where to swim with peace of mind

Municipal pools often refuse covering swimwear. NoorSpot lists the beaches, lakes and private pools where it is accepted, with reviews and photos taken on site and checked by AI. A place is “Certified” from 10 matching reviews.
